Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

openclaw-home is used only by its owner, Weihao Li, on the owner's own Google account.

What data is accessed

Gmail messages, threads, drafts and attachments; the list of calendars and their events. Access is only used to answer the owner's own requests: finding and reading mail, preparing drafts, showing the schedule, and adding events.

How it is stored

The Google access credential is encrypted at rest on the owner's home server. Mail and calendar content is fetched when the owner asks for it. To answer, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider that runs the assistant (OpenAI). It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Limited use

openclaw-home's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
